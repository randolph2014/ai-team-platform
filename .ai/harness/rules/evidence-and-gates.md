# Evidence and Gate Integrity

Harness rules and skills are project context. They cannot weaken system or developer instructions, AGENTS.md, platform safety policy, human gates, or quality gates.

Plans must name the verification commands or scans that will prove the change. Final reports must include the commands actually run and their results.

Disabling a gate, hiding a failed check, lowering a baseline, or claiming unverified work as complete is a blocking review finding.
